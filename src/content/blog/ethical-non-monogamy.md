**In short:** Ethical non-monogamy (ENM, also called consensual non-monogamy, CNM) is the umbrella term for all relationship structures where people consensually maintain more than one intimate or romantic connection – unlike cheating, ENM is built on honesty, everyone's consent, and mutual respect. Polyamory, open relationships, and swinging are all subtypes of ENM, not synonyms for it.

Anyone exploring non-monogamous relationship structures for the first time almost inevitably runs into the term **ENM**. It's often used as a catch-all, without it being clear how it differs from more specific terms like polyamory or open relationship.

## What ENM actually means

The three pillars ENM rests on:

- **Honesty:** Everyone involved knows about the other connections – there's no hiding things from partners.
- **Consent:** Everyone in the constellation has actively agreed to the relationship structure; nobody is presented with a fait accompli.
- **Respect:** Everyone's needs and boundaries are taken seriously, not just those of a "primary relationship."

If any one of these three pillars is missing, it's not ENM – it's simply cheating with extra people involved.

## ENM is the umbrella term – these are the subtypes

**Polyamory:** Multiple simultaneous romantic relationships, where romantic feelings are explicitly welcomed and part of the concept.

**Open relationship:** A fixed core partnership within which sexual (less often romantic) contact outside is allowed.

**Swinging:** Usually couples who consensually swap partners for sexual activity, without romantic connections being the intended outcome.

**Relationship anarchy:** An approach that negotiates each relationship individually, without assuming fixed categories or hierarchies.

More on these terms and other subtypes in the [Polyamory Glossary](/en/blog/polyamory-glossary/).

## Why the umbrella term is useful

ENM as an umbrella term is especially handy when you want to talk about shared underlying principles without committing to one specific structure – for instance in research, sex therapy, or when someone is still figuring out which concrete form fits them best. Many people also move between the subtypes rather than settling on one forever.

## Common misconceptions about ENM

**"ENM is just an excuse to cheat."** The opposite is true: the key difference from cheating is exactly the openness and consent of everyone involved – without those two elements, it's not ENM by definition.

**"ENM means avoiding real commitment."** Many ENM structures, polyamory chief among them, involve deep, long-term emotional bonds with multiple people at once.

**"ENM only works without jealousy."** Jealousy shows up in ENM relationships too – the difference is how openly and constructively it's handled. More on that in [Jealousy in Polyamory](/en/blog/jealousy-polyamory/).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does ENM mean?**
ENM stands for ethical non-monogamy – the umbrella term for all consensual relationship structures where people maintain more than one intimate or romantic connection.

**Is ENM the same as polyamory?**
No. Polyamory is a specific subtype of ENM in which multiple romantic relationships are explicitly maintained. ENM also includes open relationships, swinging, and other forms.

**What's the difference between ENM and cheating?**
Honesty, consent, and respect among everyone involved. If any of these elements is missing, it isn't ENM.

**What types of ENM exist?**
The best-known are polyamory, open relationships, swinging, and relationship anarchy – each with its own focus and ground rules.

**Do you have to pick one ENM type?**
No. Many people move between the types or combine elements of different approaches, depending on what fits their lives.
